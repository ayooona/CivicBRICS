Terms of Service & Public Good Policy

Guiding principles for ethical civic participation

  • Authentic Grassroots Reporting: Submissions must represent genuine civic infrastructure deficits (clean water, rural clinics, unpaved roads, power disruptions, and schools).
  • Multilingual AI Processing: Submissions in regional languages are processed via Google Gemini AI solely for translation, categorization, and objective urgency scoring.
  • Provisioned Official Access: Government accounts are pre-configured by ministries. Officials log in with their official email and password to review and resolve assigned issues.
  • Public Transparency: Infrastructure demands and resolutions are published on the open Policymaker Dashboard to ensure equitable municipal action across member regions.
